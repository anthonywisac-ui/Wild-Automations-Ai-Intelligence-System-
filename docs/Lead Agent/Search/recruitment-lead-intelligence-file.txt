Cover Page
Title: Recruitment Lead Intelligence File
Subtitle: Employer-side job search terms and hiring demand categories for global and country-wise lead generation.
Use Case: Feed these search terms into your scraper to find companies that are actively hiring and may need recruitment support.

Purpose
This file is designed to help a job-scraping system find employers who are hiring, not job seekers. When your scraper searches these terms across job boards, career pages, and public listings, it can reveal companies with active hiring needs, urgent vacancies, and recurring recruitment demand. That data can then be sold to hiring companies as leads, talent sourcing, or recruitment support.

Suggested Scraper Logic
Your scraper should prioritize searches that combine a role, location, and urgency signal. That will help you find companies with real hiring pain instead of generic listings. Job scraping platforms commonly support keyword, location, date, and job-type filters, so this type of structured list is practical to automate.

Search by job title + country.

Search by industry + country.

Search by urgency terms + country.

Search by multiple openings + country.

Search by remote / hybrid / onsite.

Search by company career pages.

Search by “hiring now” variants.

Search by “join our team” variants.

Search by local city names.

Search by contract / permanent / temporary.
