Search Intelligence Expansion Document
Purpose
Yeh document current Buyer Persona & Intent Engine ko expand karta hai taa-ke aapka system sirf business names na dhoonde, balkay listings, categories, departments, aur public comments se real buying signals bhi nikaale. Iska goal lead discovery ko zyada precise, zyada scalable, aur zyada automation-friendly banana hai.

Core Search Layers
1) Business listings
Listings woh jagah hain jahan business already structured form mein milta hai, jaise Google Maps, local directories, niche directories, marketplace listings, aur industry association pages. In sources se business name, category, location, contact details, reviews, aur sometimes website ya WhatsApp bhi mil jata hai.

2) Business categories
Categories ko sirf broad level par na dekho, balkay multiple layers mein search karo. Broad category, sub-category, service-line category, city-specific category, aur platform-specific category — yeh sab alag intent signal dete hain.

3) Departments
Departments search karna bohat useful hai kyun ke yahan operational pain zyada clearly nazar aata hai. Front desk, reception, sales, marketing, HR, operations, finance, customer support, admissions, reservations, procurement, aur business development jaise departments direct workflows aur pain points reveal karte hain.

4) Comments and reviews
Comments aur reviews sab se strong signal ho sakte hain kyun ke yeh frustration, delay, missed calls, booking issues, aur service gaps ko expose karte hain. Especially 1-star se 3-star reviews, repeated complaint themes, aur business owner replies ko analyze karna chahiye.

Suggested Engine Upgrades
Search term generator ko separate layers mein split karo: listings, categories, departments, aur comments.

Review miner add karo jo pain themes aur owner replies nikaale.

Entity normalizer use karo taake business names, categories, aur branches consistent rahen.

Intent ranking add karo taake keyword match se zyada business objective ke hisaab se ranking ho.

Signal memory maintain karo jo bataye kaun se queries best leads la rahi hain.

Query rotation use karo taake repeated patterns kam hon aur coverage zyada ho.

Department mapper banayo jo industry ke hisaab se relevant departments suggest kare.

Pain library maintain karo jo har niche ke common problems store kare.

Final Direction
Best system woh hoga jo four layers mein search kare: business listings, business categories, internal departments, aur public comments. Is structure se aap business bhi dhoondh sakte ho aur pain bhi — aur yehi combination actual high-quality lead banata hai.
